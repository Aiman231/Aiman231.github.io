Hi there! I’m Aiman Shaikh, a second-year Computer Science major at Simon Fraser University (SFU).

I’m passionate about leveraging technology to create solutions that help people, especially students, succeed in their academic journeys.

Beyond academics, I run my own business that provides educational notes and resources, making learning more accessible and efficient for students through my online store.

When I’m not coding or running my business, you can find me swimming, horseback riding, or immersed in a good book.

I graduated from WJ Mouat Secondary and am currently pursuing my Computer Science degree at SFU.

As a volunteer note-taker at SFU, I provide lecture notes for students registered with the Centre for Accessible Learning (CAL) who cannot attend classes in person. This experience has not only honed my attention to detail but also deepened my commitment to making education more accessible.
